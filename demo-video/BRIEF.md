# TanyaCCTV pitch video: production brief

## Purpose and audience

- Event: Sime Darby Property University Hackathon 2026, theme "Township of Tomorrow", **People track**
  (safety, wellbeing, community). Team: **Simebody's Gonna Win**.
- Product: **TanyaCCTV**. Tagline: *Your township's cameras, now listening.*
- Audience: judges, pitched like investors. Premium, confident, emotional, and honest.
- About 3:00-3:30, 1920x1080, 30 fps.

**One line:** TanyaCCTV turns a township's existing cameras from recorders into responders: it finds
any moment in seconds, and warns the guard before an incident happens.

## Style

- Dark and cinematic: background #07090C, orange #E8742F / #F59A5F, red #E3342F only for alarms,
  green #3DD68C for matches and tracking.
- Helvetica Neue / Inter; thin weights for statements, bold key words, wide-tracked uppercase kickers.
- Text rises with a slight blur-in; slow push-in on cards; slow zoom-ins on key app moments.
- Subtitles: white on a translucent dark box, none on the closing card.

## Sound

- Voice: warm, confident, human narrator (founder tone). ElevenLabs `eleven_multilingual_v2`,
  stability about 0.45, style about 0.25.
- Music: "Drawing the Sky", Eugenio Mininni (Mixkit free licence). The swell lands on the logo reveal;
  ducked under the voice.
- Effects: heartbeat under the collapse, low impact on "Nobody's watching", 2 beeps for warnings,
  4 alternating beeps for the alarm, whoosh on cards, key clicks while typing.

## Honesty rules

- Dramatisation: Uncle Lim and Officer Hafiz are fictional; say so on screen and in the credits.
- Never claim it detects heart attacks, criminals, identity or intent: "flags someone who collapsed and
  isn't moving", "someone at the motorbike".
- Live feeds are recordings played as live; search results in the demo are pre-analysed; the map route
  is illustrative.
- Don't imply Sime Darby uses or endorses TanyaCCTV, and don't use their logo. Only the 2009 Safe City
  fact is theirs (Sime Darby Property press release).
