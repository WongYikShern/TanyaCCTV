# Data folder

- `videos/` — put **authorised sample CCTV footage only** here (footage you own, have
  written permission to use, or that is published under a licence that allows it).
  Do not add footage of people who have not consented. These files are git-ignored
  and are never modified or deleted by the indexer.
- `frames/` — JPEG frames generated by the indexer, one folder per video
  (named after the video's SHA-256). Safe to delete; re-run the indexer to rebuild.
- `tanya.db` — SQLite database created by the indexer. Safe to delete; re-run to rebuild.
